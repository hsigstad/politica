Brazilian political data from the TSE, cleaned into relational tables. politica cleans and assembles Tribunal Superior Eleitoral (TSE) open data — candidates, campaign finance, election results, party organs, candidacy sanctions, mayoral manifestos, and registered opinion polls, 1998–2024 — into analysis-ready CSV/parquet tables usable as a relational database. The cards below group the tables by domain; each links to a per-table data page with columns, example rows, grain checks, and coverage-by-year. Individual CPFs and voter-registration numbers are masked.
