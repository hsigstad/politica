politico — deduplicated person registry

One row per person (politico_id), deduplicated across all cycles keeping the first occurrence. Carries birthplace (birth_municipio, birth_estado), demographics (gender, race, nationality, birthdate), and the titulo/CPF basis of the person key. politico_id is CPF when available, else 'T' + titulo eleitoral (see docs/data.md).
